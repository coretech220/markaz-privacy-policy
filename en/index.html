MARKAZ · PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 November 2026

Markaz can read its bundled books and perform text and semantic search on your device. Some features use online services, including shared calendar updates, notifications and YouTube. This policy explains the information involved in each case and the controls available to you.

1. About this policy

This policy applies to the current Android application Markaz — Sayyid Shouhadaa Center (مركز سيد الشهداء (ع)), package com.sayyidshouhada.center, and this privacy-policy website. “We” and “our” refer to the operator of the Markaz application, reachable through the contact address below.

The app does not require a reader account or a sign-in to use its books and devotional features. The separate private administration service is for authorised administrators; it is not a reader account system.

This policy describes the Android functionality available at the effective date. Any future iOS version will need to be reviewed against its actual permissions, SDKs and services before publication.

2. Information stored and processed on your device

The app stores settings and activity needed for its local features. These may include:

  • Appearance, font, sound and vibration preferences.
  • Saved items, bookmarks, reading positions and local search/navigation state.
  • Quiz progress and remembrance-counter sessions, counts and history.
  • The country and city you manually select, including the associated coordinates and time zone from the bundled city catalogue.
  • Cached calendar declarations and lecture metadata, plus received-notification history, receipt times and read status.

The app's text search and semantic or “AI” search run locally using bundled indexes and model data. These searches do not send your search terms to a remote AI service.

The manually selected city and the device's compass/sensor readings are used locally for day/night timing and Qibla calculations. Markaz does not request GPS or Android location permissions for these features.

3. Online calendar and lecture updates

When the app opens or returns to the foreground, it checks the Markaz service hosted on Google Firebase for shared Hijri-calendar declarations and the administrator's latest-lecture list. The same shared response is used to calculate the relevant calendar date on your device.

This update request does not include your selected city, country, search terms, bookmarks or counter history, and it does not use an app-reader account. As with an internet connection generally, the hosting provider can receive your IP address and routine technical request information, such as request time and browser/app information, for service operation and security.

Online media lists may also request publicly available YouTube feed information. Cached content and the app's local fallback calculations remain available when an online request fails.

4. Notifications

The Android notification feature uses Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) and Firebase installation services. Registration and delivery involve app-installation identifiers, notification registration tokens and technical app/device information handled by Google. Markaz uses a shared broadcast topic to deliver announcements; the app does not upload a reader profile or your local reading and counter history with this subscription.

On Android versions that require a notification permission, the app asks for that permission. On older versions, activation can occur when Android already allows notifications. You can control notification display in Android's app notification settings; the app's notification controls can open those settings or request activation where appropriate.

Turning off notification permission prevents notification display as controlled by Android. It does not itself delete a Firebase installation or registration token. Received message content, receipt times and read status are stored locally so that you can revisit messages in the notification history.

5. YouTube, cookies and external services

Lecture and media screens use YouTube-hosted thumbnails. Selecting a video loads a YouTube player and related YouTube services. These requests can reveal your IP address, device/browser information and playback interactions to Google/YouTube. YouTube may use cookies or similar technologies and may display advertising under its own policies.

The app's Android WebView accepts first-party and third-party cookies so that embedded web content can work. This means the app is not entirely cookie-free, even though its local books and searches do not need advertising trackers.

Links to the centre's website, YouTube and social or messaging services open the selected service or your browser. If you use one of those services, its own terms, privacy policy and account settings apply. Markaz does not receive your password for those services.

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • YouTube Terms of Service
  • YouTube information about user data

6. Contacting us

The current “Contact us” form asks for a message and lets you optionally include a name and reply email address. Continuing opens your email application with a draft addressed to info@sayyid-shouhadaa-center.com. You review and send the message through your email provider; the app does not silently send the form through a separate contact-form server.

If you send that email, we receive the message, any details you chose to include, and the sender information normally supplied by email. We use this information to respond to and handle your request. Your email provider also processes the email under its own policy. Please do not include sensitive information that is unnecessary for your request.

7. Android permissions and device access

The application uses internet and network-state access for its online features, vibration for optional feedback, and notification/messaging permissions and wake functionality needed for notification delivery. Notification permission is subject to the Android version and your system settings.

The app does not request access to your contacts, microphone, camera, phone location, photos or shared files. Qibla orientation uses device sensors where available, without requesting your GPS position. Selecting a country and city is manual.

8. Analytics, advertising and payments

The current Markaz application does not integrate a dedicated analytics SDK, Firebase Analytics, a crash-reporting SDK or an advertising SDK. Its Firebase notification integration is distinct from analytics.

Markaz has no in-app purchase or subscription-billing feature. This does not prevent third-party content, such as a YouTube player, from displaying its own advertising or processing information as explained above.

9. Retention, deletion and your choices

Local app data remains on your device until you use an available in-app clearing control, clear the app's storage through Android, or uninstall the app. Clearing saved items or counter history affects those records; it does not clear every category of app data. Android app-data clearing is the broader control, including locally stored notification history and preferences.

Support emails can remain in correspondence records as needed to handle the request and related records. This policy does not promise an automatic deletion period that the current service does not implement. Technical information processed by Google, YouTube, your email provider or GitHub is subject to those providers' retention practices.

You can avoid opening online media or external links, manage notifications in Android settings, and contact us about privacy or information you sent directly to us. Where applicable law provides rights to access, correction or deletion, send your request to the address below. Requests concerning data held independently by another service should also be directed to that service.

No reader account is created by Markaz, so there is no reader-account deletion process or sign-in account to close.

10. Children and families

Markaz includes religious and educational material, including previews of children's content. It does not provide child accounts or a dedicated system for collecting children's personal information. Parents and guardians should supervise children's use of email, YouTube and other external services.

If you believe a child has sent personal information to us in a support message, please contact us so that we can review the request. The availability of educational content does not remove the privacy practices of external services used from the app.

11. Security and service providers

The app uses HTTPS for its configured online services and keeps its books and local activity within app storage. It restricts access to the private administration service to authorised administrators. No internet service or device storage can be guaranteed completely secure.

Google Firebase provides hosting and messaging used by online app features; Google/YouTube provides media services; your chosen email provider handles support messages you send; and GitHub hosts this policy website. These providers may process information in countries other than your own according to their services and policies.

  • Firebase Privacy and Security information
  • GitHub General Privacy Statement

12. This privacy-policy website

This website is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. It does not add analytics scripts, tracking pixels, contact forms, cookies or externally hosted fonts. Reading a page or loading its local images still sends a normal web request to GitHub, which can process IP addresses and technical request logs under its privacy statement.

13. Changes and privacy contact

We will update this policy when the app's relevant functionality or data practices change. The effective date at the top identifies this version. Review the policy again when using a materially changed feature.
